# 40C3 style guide

`40c3-styleguide-full-latest.zip` is the style guide of the 40th Chaos
Communication Congress, from
<https://events.ccc.de/congress/2026/infos/styleguide/>.

The style guide page states:

> The design concept and assets are under CC0, so you are free to use them in
> your own creations. The used fonts, Jersey 10 and Hack have their own
> licenses. Those are included in the fonts folder.

The font licences are in the zip under `fonts/` and, for the bundled copies of
the fonts, next to them in `../fonts/`.
